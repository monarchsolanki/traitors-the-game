Scope: the whole app. Player phones and big screen are Experience. Host and co-host panels are Operate: the world lends type, palette, density and one signature move, and standard controls stay standard.

Audience and job: a dozen friends in a dim villa, phones held low, a TV across the room, the host at a Mac. Guests must always know what to tap. Exclusivity comes from being on the list, never from being hard to use. Keep every function, rule and piece of content; the only approved copy change is the home date line, now "By invitation only".

Memorable moment: at "Night falls" every phone and the TV change colour together, and the room feels the house lights drop.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A private night at a Bombay Deco palace: a tower sign you are let past, your portrait on the wall, house lights that change for the whole room at once. It refuses black and gold, centred logo over a form, and round avatars in dark cards.

OWN-WORLD: Peacock lacquer ground by day, oxblood at the Circle, midnight indigo at night; rose sandstone accent, nickel hairlines, terrazzo ivory ink, vermilion only for danger and Traitors. No gold anywhere. Stepped corners on every frame, triple speed lines, tall Limelight sign lettering, Federo titles, Jost body. Portraits are tall stepped plates.

STORY: The guest sees they were invited, finds their own portrait, keeps a secret behind lift doors, and votes without thinking about the interface. The room reads the TV as theatre. The host reads a precise panel.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone home: a rose tower bleeds off the left edge with a stepped top and TRAITORS stacked down its full height. Right of it, "The Game" and "By invitation only" at top; room code, Join the game and I'm the host at the thumb. TV lobby: same tower left, QR and code centre, portrait wall right. Host: top bar, rail, controls column.

FORM: Maharaja Deco, position 5 of my ordered list. seed:11f21e783abb2fdc. Raises kept from the declined hand: one module (the corner step sets every gap); the Circle is a real ring on the TV with each vote drawn across it, one at a time; vast empty ground, nothing labelled twice; type as structure on the TV; only the current moment at full strength. Signature interactions: house lights across all screens; lift doors on hold-to-reveal; votes drawn across the ring. Motion grammar: one slow colour change per act, one-way exponential ease-out, everything off under reduced motion.
